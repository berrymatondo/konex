Source evidence for executed price, premium / discount and settlement FX.
Settlement FX calculation: the app fixes the CDF obligation at the trade-date rate and retranslates it at the payment-date rate. Calculated effect = base settlement USD − (base settlement USD × trade-date FX ÷ payment-date FX). A positive result is favourable. An optional override supersedes the calculated value and remains visible in the audit trail.
